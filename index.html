Connect YouTube Music

YouTube Music has no public API that allows third-party sites to stream your library, and Google does not issue OAuth scopes for playback. So this player can't legally stream your YT Music account.

Instead, use + Add files to load your own audio (MP3/WAV/OGG) — they play instantly with full controls, playlists and favorites.
